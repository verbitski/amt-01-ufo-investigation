# Start with one small contribution

AMT-01 is an unresolved sighting near Antalya in summer 2004. You can help by checking one calculation, comparing one possible explanation, or making one sentence clearer.

**New here? [See the reconstructions](https://amt01.vercel.app/#reconstructions) or read the [short account](../01-case-amt01/00-case-summary.md).** The images were made after the event from memory. They are not event photographs or independent evidence.

Choose one task below. A partial answer with a source and an honest limit is useful; you do not have to solve the whole case.

## Check the speed estimate

**Question:** Does the published speed range follow from the stated path and duration, and how sensitive is it to either estimate?

- **Start with:** [Geometric and kinematic analysis, sections 1, 2, and 6](../02-derived-analysis/00-geometric-and-kinematic-analysis.md).
- **Inputs:** a later estimated path of 40–50 metres and a recalled duration of 12–15 seconds.
- **Do:** calculate the limiting speeds in metres per second and kilometres per hour. Then change one input, clearly labeling your chosen value as a hypothetical scenario.
- **A useful answer:** the equation, units, arithmetic, and a few sentences explaining which assumption matters. Remember that path and duration come from the same memory and may be correlated. Correct arithmetic does not confirm either estimate.
- **Share it:** [discuss the calculation on Discord](https://discord.gg/JVvHf5cXhs). For a result ready for structured review, use the [analysis form](https://github.com/verbitski/amt-01-ufo-investigation/issues/new?template=04-analysis-or-hypothesis-proposal.yml&title=%5BAnalysis%5D%3A%20Check%20the%20speed%20estimate&question=Does%20the%20published%20speed%20range%20follow%20from%20the%20stated%20path%20and%20duration%3F&related_records=docs%2F02-derived-analysis%2F00-geometric-and-kinematic-analysis.md%2C%20sections%201%2C%202%2C%20and%206).

## Compare one known object

**Question:** Could a real object or platform available by summer 2004 explain a specific part of the written account?

- **Start with:** the [witness record](../01-case-amt01/01-full-observation-record.md) and [existing alternative explanations](../03-hypotheses/01-evaluated-alternatives.md).
- **Find:** one original, dated source documenting the candidate. Link a museum record, manufacturer document, archive, or other primary source; do not upload third-party media without permission.
- **Compare:** one feature that fits and one that does not. State whether your comparison depends on uncertain distance, lighting, size, or memory.
- **A useful answer:** “My candidate is …; this source shows …; it might explain …; it does not explain …; a useful next check would be …”. Similarity to a reconstruction alone is not evidence of identity.
- **Share it:** [discuss your candidate on Discord](https://discord.gg/JVvHf5cXhs). A developed comparison can go through the [analysis form](https://github.com/verbitski/amt-01-ufo-investigation/issues/new?template=04-analysis-or-hypothesis-proposal.yml&title=%5BAnalysis%5D%3A%20Compare%20one%20known%20object).

## Make one detail clearer

**Question:** Which sentence, source link, or label makes the public account harder to understand or check?

- **Start with:** the [case summary](../01-case-amt01/00-case-summary.md) or the [website](https://amt01.vercel.app/).
- **Do:** point to one exact passage or broken link. Suggest wording or a replacement source and explain what it fixes.
- **A useful answer:** the page link, the original sentence or link, your proposed correction, and a short reason. Preserve the difference between what was remembered, later estimated, calculated, and remains unknown.
- **Share it:** use the [documentation correction form](https://github.com/verbitski/amt-01-ufo-investigation/issues/new?template=02-documentation-or-data-correction.yml&title=%5BCorrection%5D%3A%20Clarify%20one%20detail), or [ask on Discord](https://discord.gg/JVvHf5cXhs) if you are unsure.

## Before you share

Discord and GitHub are public. Keep personal witness reports, identifying information, exact private locations, and sensitive media out of them. For your own observation or a privacy request, use the [private witness form](https://tally.so/r/5BgZMb).

When you are ready to submit research or code, the [contribution guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) explains source checks, AI disclosure, licenses, DCO sign-off for commits, and review. Discussion does not automatically become part of the research record.
